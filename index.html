Common questions

Will I lose my goals if I get a new phone?
Not if you sign in with Apple or Google: your goals are backed up and come back when you sign in on the new phone. Guests’ data lives on the one phone.

Reminders aren’t showing up.
Check Settings → Notifications → Sproutine and make sure notifications are allowed. You can change the reminder style in the app under ☰ → Edit goals & reminders.

How do I change a goal just for today?
Tap the goal on the Today screen, choose “Just one day”, and pick a new time (any time before its usual slot).

Can I take a week off without losing my streak?
Yes: ☰ → Edit schedule → Pause this week.

How do I delete my account?
☰ → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account (or guest backup) and your backed-up data.
