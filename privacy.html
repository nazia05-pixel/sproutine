Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

The short version: Sproutine keeps your goals and progress so it can back them up and bring them to your other devices. We don’t sell your data, we don’t show ads, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

This policy explains what the Sproutine app (“Sproutine”, “we”, “us”) collects, why, and the choices you have. Sproutine is made by an independent developer. Questions? Email nazia05@gmail.com.

What we collect

WhatWhy
Account details: if you sign in with Apple or Google, your account ID, email address and first name as shared by Apple or Google. With Apple’s “Hide My Email”, we only get a private relay address.To create your account, sign you in, and greet you by name.
Guest ID: if you continue as a guest, a random anonymous ID (no name or email).To back up your goals without an account.
Your routine: the goals you create (titles, emojis, days, times, notes), when you tick them off, your streaks and your app settings (such as reminder style).This is the app: to show your day, your progress and Sprout’s reactions, and to back it all up.
A security check: when you continue as a guest, hCaptcha checks that you’re a person and not a bot, using technical information from your device (such as how the check page behaves and your IP address).To stop automated abuse of guest sign-ups.

We don’t collect your location, contacts, photos, health data, advertising identifiers, or anything from other apps. Sproutine has no ads and no third-party analytics.

Where it’s stored

  • On your device: your routine is kept on your phone so the app works offline.
  • In the cloud: a copy is backed up to our database, hosted by Supabase. Each account can only read and change its own data; this is enforced by the database itself.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell or rent your data, and we don’t share it with advertisers or data brokers. We only use these service providers to run the app:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and backup (privacy policy).
  • Apple and Google: only if you choose to sign in with them (Apple, Google).
  • hCaptcha: the security check for guests (privacy policy).
  • Expo: delivers app updates; this involves your device contacting Expo’s servers (privacy policy).

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your device as local notifications. Their content isn’t sent to us. You can turn them off in your iPhone’s Settings.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

  • We keep your data while you have an account.
  • Delete my account (in the app’s ☰ menu) permanently deletes your account, or your guest backup, and all your backed-up data from our database, and clears the app on your device.
  • You can also email nazia05@gmail.com to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it.

Children

Sproutine isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access to each account’s data is restricted to that account. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to certain uses. Email us and we’ll help.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
